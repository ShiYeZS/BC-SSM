# Third-Party Notices

## CAMELS Data Utilities

The data-loading utilities in `data.py` are adapted from the Apache-2.0 CAMELS
utilities distributed with Kratzert et al. (2019),
https://doi.org/10.1029/2019WR026065.

BC-SSM packaging changes include direct text loading, indexed sequence windows,
and date-preserving evaluation. The license is provided in `Apache-2.0.txt`.

## NeuralHydrology

BC-SSM evaluation imports metric implementations from NeuralHydrology 1.13.0.
The dependency's license is provided in `neuralhydrology-BSD-3-Clause.txt`.
